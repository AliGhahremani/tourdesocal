The Green Jersey Race

Both volume jerseys are cumulative, so the totals themselves are useless on a chart: eighteen miles separate first and second after eight months, which is one pixel on a three thousand mile axis. This plots the gap to whoever is leading. The leader rides along the top; everyone else hangs below. It plays on arrival, and you can drag the slider to any day or hover the chart to read it.
